# Website Plan – Lapuan Lepakot ry

## 1. Goal and Must-Haves

The goal is to create a simple, clear and mobile-friendly website for Lapuan Lepakot ry, a floorball club in Lapua.

The website will make it easier for players, parents and new members to find important club information without searching through different WhatsApp groups.

The website must include:

- Upcoming match schedules with dates, times and locations.
- Past match results.
- Training schedules for different teams.
- Information about junior, men's and women's teams.
- Information about joining the club.
- A free trial practice registration form.
- Contact information for the club.
- Easy navigation between all website pages.

The website will use the following brand colours:

- Text: `#2e2a24`
- Background: `#f3ead8`
- Accent: `#8a5a2b`

The design will have a warm, old-paper style with brown accents.

## 2. Visitors

The website is mainly designed for three types of visitors.

**Parents and guardians**

They want to quickly check their children's match schedules, training times and locations.

**Current players**

They need information about upcoming matches, results, training schedules and club announcements.

**New players and families**

They want to learn about the club, available teams and how to join or try a practice session.

## 3. Content Plan

### Home – `index.html`

Purpose: Welcome visitors and provide quick access to important information.

Content:
- Short introduction to Lapuan Lepakot.
- Upcoming matches.
- Links to teams and match information.
- Invitation to join the club or try a practice session.

Main heading: "Welcome to Lapuan Lepakot"

### About – `about.html`

Purpose: Introduce the club and its activities.

Content:
- About the club.
- Junior, men's and women's teams.
- Club values and activities.
- Information for people interested in joining.

Main heading: "About Our Club"

### Matches and Training – `listing.html`

Purpose: Show important schedules and results in one place.

Content:
- Upcoming match schedules.
- Past match results.
- Weekly training schedules.
- Match dates, times and locations.

Main heading: "Matches and Training"

### Contact and Join – `contact.html`

Purpose: Help visitors contact the club and register for a free trial practice.

Content:
- Club contact information.
- Free trial practice registration form.
- Player name and email fields.
- Team selection and optional message.

Main heading: "Contact and Join Us"

The schedules and results used in the first version will be sample information until the club provides confirmed details.

## 4. Three Real Reference Websites

### 1. Salibandyliitto
Website: https://salibandy.fi/

What works:
- Provides a wide range of floorball information.
- Organises information into clear sections.
- Includes competition and player-related information.

What could be improved for our project:
- A smaller local club website should have fewer sections and simpler navigation.

What we will use:
- Clear organisation of floorball information and competition-related content.

### 2. F-liiga
Website: https://fliiga.com/

What works:
- Focuses on floorball competitions.
- Provides information for followers of the sport.
- Uses a sports-focused identity.

What could be improved for our project:
- The amount of information may be unnecessary for a small volunteer-run club.

What we will use:
- A clear sports theme and easy-to-find match information.

### 3. TPS Salibandy
Website: https://www.tpssalibandy.fi/

What works:
- Focuses specifically on a floorball club.
- Introduces club activities and teams.
- Uses a recognisable club identity.

What could be improved for our project:
- Our website should focus especially on parents and visitors who need schedules and joining information quickly.

What we will use:
- A club-focused structure and clear introduction to team activities.

## 5. Tone and Key Messages

The website should sound friendly, welcoming, clear and informative.

It should be easy to understand for parents, players and new visitors.

Three key messages:

1. Everyone is welcome to become part of Lapuan Lepakot.
2. Find match schedules and training information easily.
3. Come and try floorball with us!

## 6. What We Need from the Customer

To complete the website accurately, we need:

- Official club logo and photographs.
- Confirmed match schedules and locations.
- Latest match results.
- Training times and locations for each team.
- Contact person's name, email and phone number.
- Information about joining the club.
- Details about free trial practices.
- Confirmation of how registration information should be sent to the club.

Until the information is provided, sample schedules and placeholder content will be used.

The free trial form will first be presented as a demonstration. The club's IT partner can later connect it to the club's email system.

## 7. Next Steps

After the customer approves the plan:

1. Build the four HTML pages: Home, About, Matches and Training, and Contact.
2. Create consistent navigation and page structure.
3. Add the confirmed club information, schedules and photographs.
4. Apply the club's colours and mobile-friendly design.
5. Test all links, forms and pages.
6. Publish the website and collect feedback from the club.

The final goal is to provide Lapuan Lepakot with a simple website that makes club information easier to access for everyone.
